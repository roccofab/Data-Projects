# Farm Analytics
### Data analytics project that cover the entire data lifecycle for agricultural analysis of a tertiary sector company specialized in oil production.The project begins by creating a dataset containing **daily weather data** retrieved by using **openMeteo API**, then through **Data Simulation Engines** it generates random **Production and Consumption data**. Various **exploratory and advanced analyses** are then performed on the Meteorological-Production-Consumption data. The project also includes a **Dashboard** created in Streamlit and Altair. Modular architecture with structured datasets and automated testing.

## Features:
- **Open-Meteo API integration:** Daily weather fetch from 2000 to current date and yearly aggregations.

- **Simulation engine (production and consumption):** realistic generation of historical series of yields, plants, costs and operating expenses.Weather Data has a strong impact on Production and Consumption Data.

- **Daily weather, production and consumption dataset.**

- **Data cleaning and validation.**

- **Exploratory and Advanced Analysis:** trends, economic/agronomic KPIs, weather-yield correlations, and inter-annual comparisons.

- **Cost and Performance Metrics:** cost per ton/kg, cost per liter of oil, profit/ha, annual growth.

- **Data Visualization by using static charts**

- **Web Dashboard Streamlit + Altair:** interactive data visualization, time filters, comparisons and key insights.

- **Testing (unit and integration):** MockEngine simulates the database connection, avoiding real interactions with the database. Further tests were performed to verify the reliability of the data simulated by the random data simulation engines.

- **Modular architecture:** Clear separation between data acquisition, simulation, EDA, and web applications.

## 🧰 Tools Used:
- Python, Pandas, Numpy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Streamlit, Altair, pytest,unittest, MagicMock.

- **Other Technologies:** MySQL, SQLAlchemy, HTTP/API(requests, Open-Meteo API).

## 📦 Dataset:
1. **weather_data:**
    - **date(datetime(yyyy-dd-mm))**

    - **temperature_2m(float)**: daily temperature detected at 2 meters above the ground.
    
    - **precipitation(float)**: daily precipitations.
    
    - **wind_speed_10m(float)**: wind speed detected at 10 meters above the ground.
    
    - **daylight duration(float)**

2. **production_data:**
    - **year**(int)

    - **plants_counter(int)**: number of plants.

    - **harvest_days(int)**: number of days spent harvesting the olives.

    - **gross_qty(decimal(10,2))**: gross quantity of olives harvested.

    - **net_qty(decimal(10,2))**: net quantity of olives harvested(75-80% of gross_qty).

    - **oil_yield(decimal(10,2))**: 15-20% of net_qty in kg
    
    - **oil_yield_pct(decimal(5,2))**: 15-20% of net_qty in percentage.
    
    - **oil_revenue(decimal(10,2))**: total yearly turnover generated by the sale of oil, the base price per kg of oil is 5.0, but If the year net olive harvest < average olive harvest and year total consumption > average total consumption then increase the oil price per kg of a random value between 2 and 3.
    
    - **surface-area_in_m2(decimal(10,2))**: each plants occupies 18m^2, the total yearly surface area is the product between the number of plants and 18.

3. **consumption_data:**
    - **year(int)**

    - **irrigation_required_m3(decimal(10,2))**: annual irrigation water requirements in m^3.

    - **irrigation_expenses(decimal(10,2))**: irrigation expenses for each year calculate by multiplying the amount of water required in m^3 and water price per m^3(0.3).

    - **fertilizer_required_kg(decimal(12,2))**: annual fertilizer required in kg is calculated  by multiplying the dosage of fertilizer per m2(0.03) with the surface area in m2.

    - **fertilizer_costs(decimal(10,2))**:total annual fertilization cost is calculated from the fertilizer costs per hectare

    - **pesticide_required_kg(decimal(12,2))**: the pesticide required in kg is calculated using a dosage of 0.01 kg/m2.

    - **pesticide_costs(decimal(12,2))**: total annual pesticide cost is calculated from the required amount and the cost per ton.

    - **maintenance_expenses(decimal(12,2))**: base expenses(3000) + surface maintenance expenses + plants expenses.

## 🔍 Findings and Key Insights:
- **Yield/ha:** net_yield_per_hectare typically between ~4.7k and ~8.6k (2023 max net yield(~8.38k); 2004 min net yield(~4.73k)).

- **Oil yield/ha:** around ~0.83–1.63 ton/ha (peak 2023).

- **Oil yield trend:** limited year-on-year variations; positive peaks in 2011, 2014, 2016, and 2023.

- **Profit Trend:** 
    - **Top Profit Years:** 2023 (profit ~817.6k€), 2020(profit ~757.4k), 2012(profit ~712.7k), 2001(profit ~702.7k), 2017(profit ~656.4k).

    - **Top Year:** 2023-year of maximum revenue, with expenses distributed across irrigation (~€46.6k), fertilization (~€20.9k), maintenance (~€50.3k), pesticides (~€11.6k).

    - **Lowest Profit Years:** 2004(profit ~273.2k), 2018(profit ~362.03k), 2002(profit ~372.6k), 2008(profit ~393.7k), 2022(profit ~418.7k).

    - **Profit/ha:** >5k€, with peaks ~7k€ (2023).

- **Extreme Weather Years:** 2004, 2017, 2018, 2022.

- **Average Quantity in kg of Oil Extracted per Plants:** >1.6kg, with peaks ~2.93(2023).

- **Expenses Trend:**
    - **Most Expensive Years:** 2021(expenses ~187.98k), 2024(expenses ~186.11k), 2000(expenses ~183.75k), 2025(expenses ~179k), 2017(expenses ~176.56k).

    - **Least Expensive Years:** 2009(expenses ~103.61k), 2010(expenses ~104.1k), 2022(expenses ~107.39k),2007(expenses ~122.28k) 2006(expenses ~124.77k).

    - **Cost to Produce 1 Ton of Net Yield:** ~132.9 (2023), typical ~170–220.

    - **Cost to Produce 1kg of Net Yield:** ~0.13–0.29 €/kg.

    - **Cost to Produce 1kg of Oil:**  ~0.68–1.63 €/L; min 2023 (0.68 €/L).

- **Area and harvest:** Gradually increasing area; harvest days with slight fluctuations, without systematic impacts.


## 📊 Statistical Relationship Analysis:
- **Precipitation-yield** correlations are almost zero (−0.01/−0.00).

- Moderately negative **precipitations vs. oil revenues (≈ −0.42)**: wetter years tend to lead to lower revenues.

- **Annual weather trend**: Temperatures have increased slightly in recent years; precipitation and wind have seen mixed results.

## Download and Usage:
### Requirements:
- Python 3.x or later
- MySQL or SQLite
- Internet Connection for Open‑Meteo API
- IDE(optional)
- Windows/Linux/macOS

### Download and SetUp Enviroment:

- Log-In to your **MySQL Shell** account and create the database:

       CREATE DATABASE agri_sud;

- Credentials Configuration (.env):

    - Create an .env file in the project root with the variables read from src/data_acquisition/DBConnection.py:

            DB_HOST=localhost
            DB_PORT=3306
            DB_USER=your_username
            DB_PASSWORD=your_password
            DB_NAME=agri_sud

- Install Dependencies: 

      pip install -r dependencies.txt 

- Populate Tables:

        python -c "from src.data_acquisition.save_production_data import generate_production_data_table; generate_production_data_table()"
        python -c "from src.data_acquisition.save_consumption_cost_data import generate_consumption_table; generate_consumption_table()"
        python src/data_acquisition/save_weather_data.py   # quando vuoi aggiungere il meteo

    Alternatively, simply run the **Main.py** file

- You can launch the Dashboard locally:
    - Open your prompt and go to the root folder.

    - Type:

             streamlit run App/Dashboard.py
